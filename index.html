About

Welcome to my webpage!

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Toronto Metropolitan University, an Assistant Professor of Economics (Status only) at the University of Toronto, and a member of the Centre for Economic Evidence on Migration (CEEM).

I am a labour economist focusing on the economics of immigration. My research explores how employer wage-setting power generates pay penalties for immigrants and examines the broader, general equilibrium effects of immigration on the labour market.

I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Toronto in 2025. I also hold an MA in Economics from the University of Michigan, a BSc in Financial Economics from the University of Toronto, and a BA in Criminology from Toronto Metropolitan University.
